1. `hero-flames` (landscape) — Realistic open fire flames isolated on pure solid black (#000000), crisp fast shutter. Orange, amber and yellow flames with deep red tips (#a3270c) and a hot pale core (#ffe2a0) near the base, a few small flying sparks. The flames rise from one straight horizontal line along the very bottom edge of the frame and span the full width. Left to right: a tall, dense cluster of licking flames in the left third (reaching about 70% of the frame height); a low, short band of small flames across the middle (about 20% of the height); a medium cluster in the right third (about 50% of the height). Every flame tip ends inside the frame against black. No logs, no grill, no wood, no smoke, no ground, no reflections.

2. `fire-corner` (landscape) — Fire rising from the bottom-left corner only, on pure solid black (#000000). Deep red-orange flame tongues (#a3270c, #d63b17, #ff7a1a, hot core #ffe2a0 at the base), a soft orange heat haze hugging the bottom-left edge, and dozens of small streaky sparks drifting up and to the right with slight motion blur. The fire mass stays within the bottom-left 45% of the width and 55% of the height. Everything beyond the middle of the frame, and the whole top-right half, is perfectly black. No smoke columns, no objects, no ground.

3. `spark-sprite` (landscape) — A reference sheet of EIGHT separate fire sparks on pure solid black (#000000), arranged in two rows of four, each one isolated with wide black space around it (they are cut apart later, so they must not touch or overlap, and nothing may sit between them). The eight sparks: (1) a tiny white-hot round spark; (2) a soft round orange ember with a halo; (3) a thin vertical spark streak, bright head at the top, fading tail below; (4) a longer thin streak, slightly tilted; (5) an irregular flake of glowing ash with a darker edge; (6) two sparks close together; (7) a dim cooling deep-red ember; (8) a small forked crackling spark. Colour ramp: #ffe2a0 core, then #ff7a1a, then #a3270c falloff. Each spark is small, about 8% of the image width. No grid lines, no labels, no numbers.

4. `spark-field` (landscape) — Floating fire sparks and glowing embers on pure solid black (#000000). Hundreds of tiny orange, amber and yellow-white sparks: sharp pinpoints, short curved motion-blurred streaks drifting upward and slightly sideways, and 3–4 larger soft out-of-focus bokeh embers. Density is highest along the far left and far right edges and in the bottom corners. The central 50% of the frame is almost empty black. No flames, no smoke.
