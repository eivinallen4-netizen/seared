# Batch A — hero, large plates, corn, builder bases

Read `assets-src/briefs/style-guide.md` and look at the mockup image `websiite refrences/landinpagestryle direcctiion.png` before generating anything. Use your built-in image generation tool. Do not edit any code in this repository; only write files under `assets-src/`.

Generate these assets (ids are the file names):

1. `hero-tray` — the hero. A large open black clamshell (lid up behind) loaded with: a split, broiled lobster tail with butter-garlic glaze, two or three grilled frenched lamb chops with bones angled up, creamy Cajun Alfredo penne underneath, and a charred grilled lemon half. Closely follow the mockup's hero tray. No checker liner in this one. Make it the most detailed, hero-quality image of the set; generate at the tool's largest size (landscape-ish container is fine, keep full container visible).
2. `plate-steak` — sliced seared ribeye-style steak (medium, pink center, dark crust) fanned over Cajun Alfredo penne, checker liner.
3. `plate-chicken` — grilled Cajun chicken breast, sliced, over half Cajun Alfredo penne / half seasoned yellow rice (two clear halves), checker liner.
4. `plate-shrimp` — a generous portion of blackened Cajun shrimp (tails on) over Cajun Alfredo penne, checker liner.
5. `plate-salmon` — one blackened Cajun salmon fillet (crisp crust, flaky pink interior visible at an edge) over seasoned yellow rice, checker liner.
6. `plate-lamb-chop` — three grilled frenched lamb chops, bones up, over Cajun Alfredo penne, checker liner.
7. `plate-filet-mignon` — two thick seared filet mignon medallions (one cut to show a pink center) over half penne / half yellow rice, checker liner.
8. `corn-loaded-cajun` — loaded Cajun corn on the cob: 3 grilled cob halves with char marks, Cajun butter, crema, grated parmesan, Cajun spice dust, sauce drizzle, parsley, in a black clamshell with checker liner.
9. `base-penne` — the open black clamshell with checker liner holding ONLY Cajun Alfredo penne (no protein), level, with room on top for proteins to be layered on later.
10. `base-rice` — same container, same angle, ONLY seasoned yellow rice.
11. `base-half-half` — same container, same angle, half Cajun Alfredo penne / half seasoned yellow rice.

Items 9–11 must share an identical container, angle and scale so they can be swapped in place in the UI.

Write `assets-src/food/manifest-a.json`. When finished, print a short summary listing every file and any asset you could not get right.
