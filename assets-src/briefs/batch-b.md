## Tacos — open black clamshell, checker liner, exactly TWO tacos side by side, same angle and scale for all five
Every taco: Parmesan-crusted tortilla, sautéed bell pepper strips, red onions, the protein, signature orange-pink Seared sauce drizzle, a little chopped herb. Make the protein clearly identifiable in each.

1. `taco-chicken` — chopped grilled Cajun chicken.
2. `taco-steak` — diced seared steak, pink centers.
3. `taco-shrimp` — whole blackened Cajun shrimp (tails off).
4. `taco-lamb-chop` — sliced grilled lamb chop meat, with one small frenched rib bone poking out of each taco for recognisability.
5. `taco-lobster-tail` — chunks of buttery red-and-white lobster tail meat.

## Protein cutouts — NO container, the protein alone, transparent background, same three-quarter angle and lighting, centered with margin. These are layered on top of a pasta/rice tray in the UI, so they should look like a portion that has just been lifted off a plate (no plate, no sauce puddle, no garnish outside the food).
6. `protein-steak` — a fanned row of 5–6 slices of seared steak, medium.
7. `protein-chicken` — a sliced grilled Cajun chicken breast, slices fanned.
8. `protein-shrimp` — a cluster of 6–8 blackened Cajun shrimp, tails on.
9. `protein-salmon` — one blackened Cajun salmon fillet.
10. `protein-lamb-chop` — two or three crossed grilled frenched lamb chops.
11. `protein-filet-mignon` — two seared filet mignon medallions, one cut showing pink.
12. `protein-lobster-tail` — one split broiled lobster tail in its shell, butter glaze.
